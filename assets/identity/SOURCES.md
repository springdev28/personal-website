# Asterisk identity assets

Original SVG exports retrieved from [Bahar Yüksel · Asterisk Identity](https://www.figma.com/design/MD0tWSRuFlPtpnO7BfRBPr/Bahar-Yuksel-Asterisk-Identity?node-id=2-63) on 2026-10-21.

| Asset | Figma master | Asset group |
| --- | --- | --- |
| asterisk-ink.svg | 2:66 | 2:67 |
| asterisk-iris.svg | 2:71 | 2:72 |
| asterisk-white.svg | 2:77 | 2:78 |

The SVG files are unmodified. Intrinsic dimensions: 283.162 × 245.226. Each is placed in a 256 × 256 clipped symbol frame using the master group's original offset (−13.581, 14.333), then scaled proportionally by its wrapper.

Identity colors: paper #F4F3ED, white #FFFFFF, ink #171915, iris #433AF2, muted #75776E. Typography: Space Grotesk and Manrope.

The root `favicon.svg` reuses the three original path geometries with a cyan fill (#51e4db), the original master offset, and proportional padding on a dark-purple (#160d23) square. `favicon.png`, `favicon.ico`, and `apple-touch-icon.png` are raster exports of that logo for browser and search compatibility.
